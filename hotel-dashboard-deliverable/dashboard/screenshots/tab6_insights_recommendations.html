Business Insights and Recommendations

Notice: Static content: based on the full project corpus; not affected by the global filters. Findings are associations, not causes. Success is a rating-based proxy (rating ≥ 4.0). About 63% of hotels are synthetic; text mining covers real hotels only.

Section A: Business Insights

1. Price and Amenity Count Alone Say Little About Guest Ratings
  • Across the combined data, room price has only a weak linear correlation with customer ratings (r = 0.086), and amenity count shows a weak negative correlation (r = −0.134). Among real hotels alone, both are weak and positive (r = 0.182 and r = 0.112).
Insight: Higher prices and more amenities do not automatically translate into better guest satisfaction.
Source: EDA tab
2. Operational Failures Are the Biggest Sources of Dissatisfaction
  • Cleanliness accounts for 1,497 negative reviews (30.7%), followed by rude or unhelpful staff (992; 20.4%).
  • Bedding (55.7% negative sentiment), bathrooms (52.0%) and air conditioning (50.8%) show particularly high dissatisfaction.
Source: Text Mining tab
3. Customer Perception of Value Depends on the Experience Delivered
  • 29.9% of 3,565 price/value-related reviews are negative, compared with an overall negative sentiment rate of 18.6%.
Insight: Guests are more likely to express dissatisfaction when the price does not match their experience.
Source: Text Mining tab
4. Hotel Performance Varies Across Categories and Markets
  • Success rates range from 69.7% for 1-star hotels to 92.0% for 5-star hotels.
  • In the combined data, Goa records 88.6% success, while Hyderabad records 72.0%.
Insight: Performance should be benchmarked against hotel category and local market expectations.
Source: EDA tab
5. Amenity Strategy Requires Complementarity, Not Just Quantity
  • Wi-Fi is present in 95.38% of hotels in the Apriori dataset; air conditioning in 91.19%.
  • The combination Business Center + Pool -> Spa has a lift of 6.19 and 74.02% confidence.
Insight: Reliable essential amenities are baseline expectations, while premium facilities may be more meaningful in complementary bundles.
Source: Association Rules tab
6. Predictive Analytics Can Support Better Decisions
  • The Random Forest model achieves 81.34% accuracy, with a 0.6571 Macro-F1 score.
  • Price, review count and amenity count are its leading predictive features.
Insight: Hotel-level prediction, supported by customer-feedback analysis, can help identify properties that need attention.
Source: Predictive Models tab

Section B: Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize Operational Quality
High Priority
  • Introduce digital housekeeping checklists and room inspections before guest check-in.
  • Schedule preventive maintenance for bathrooms, plumbing, air conditioning and bedding.
Measure: Monthly cleanliness and maintenance-related complaint rates.
Based on Insight 2
2. Strengthen Staff Service and Complaint Resolution
High Priority
  • Train front-desk and service teams in communication, complaint handling and service recovery.
  • Empower staff to resolve routine guest issues promptly.
Measure: Staff-related negative sentiment and service complaint frequency.
Based on Insight 2
3. Align Room Pricing with Perceived Value
High Priority
  • Communicate inclusions and total charges clearly to avoid price surprises.
  • Benchmark room prices against comparable local hotels and ensure room condition supports the price charged.
Measure: Negative sentiment in price/value reviews.
Based on Insight 3
4. Ensure Reliability of Essential Amenities
High Priority
  • Regularly test Wi-Fi, air conditioning and parking facilities, which are among the least positively reviewed amenities.
  • Prioritize maintaining existing facilities before investing in additional amenities.
Measure: Amenity-related complaints and equipment failure reports.
Based on Insights 2 and 5
5. Invest in Amenities Strategically
Medium Priority
  • Evaluate complementary amenities before investing in premium facilities such as spas.
  • Use association-rule evidence alongside local demand, investment costs and expected utilization.
Measure: Amenity utilization and incremental revenue.
Based on Insight 5
6. Adopt Data-Driven Performance Monitoring
Medium Priority
  • Use the interactive dashboard to monitor ratings, review sentiment, pricing, hotel category and city-level performance.
  • Use the Random Forest model to flag properties for further investigation, not as a substitute for managerial judgement.
  • Encourage genuine post-stay feedback and track recurring customer concerns.
Measure: Rating trends, review volume and negative sentiment over time.
Based on Insights 4 and 6
Overall Strategy: Fix operational weaknesses first, deliver value consistent with pricing, maintain essential amenities, and invest in differentiated offerings only when supported by evidence.
