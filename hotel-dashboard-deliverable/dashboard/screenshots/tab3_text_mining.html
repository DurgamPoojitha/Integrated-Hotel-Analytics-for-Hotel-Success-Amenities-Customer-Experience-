Key Text Mining Takeaways:

  • Overall Sentiment: 76.3% of reviews are VADER positive, 18.6% negative, 5.1% neutral. VADER achieves 87.37% agreement with customer star ratings (92.73% on clear binary signals).
  • Primary Pain Points: Cleanliness / hygiene is the #1 complaint driver (30.7% of negative reviews), followed by Rude / unhelpful staff (20.4%) and Poor maintenance (15.3%).
  • Primary Delight Drivers: Location (84.9% positive), Food (82.9% positive), and Breakfast (79.3% positive) drive the highest customer satisfaction.
